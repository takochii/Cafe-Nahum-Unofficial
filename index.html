Our Story

A little café built for big ideas.

Cafe Nahum started as a simple wish: a warm place where students, readers, and remote workers could feel at home. We put good coffee on one table, honest bakes on another, and shelves of books within reach. Come for the caffeine, stay because the chair is comfy and the light feels like a hug.
